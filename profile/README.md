# 👋 Ulink Deep Learning Club

Welcome to the Ulink Deep Learning Club GitHub! We are a student-led, interest-driven deep learning community based at Ulink College of Shanghai.

We believe that the best way to learn deep learning is not through passive, one-way lectures, but through curiosity, experimentation, and building from first principles.

## Our Philosophy: Intuition First, Jargon Later

When beginners approach deep learning, they are often met with a wall of academic terminology—PAN/FPN, bottlenecks, residual connections, annealing—without understanding why these concepts exist or what problems they solve. We believe that early understanding must take priority over rigorous precision.

Our materials are designed as a progressive "knowledge list" rather than an exhaustive encyclopedia. We use:

- Everyday analogies to build intuition before diving into math.

- Developmental context to trace how architectures evolved and what problems they solved.

- The "Three Questions": What is it? Why does it exist/work? So what?

## What We Are Building

To support our learning and research, we are building an open-source ecosystem:

- Deep Learning Curriculum: A comprehensive, bilingual (Chinese/English) guide built with Sphinx + MyST Markdown, covering calculus fundamentals, backpropagation, CNNs, Vision Transformers (ViTs), and model deployment. (English translation is currently in progress by our members!)

- mnist-helloworld: A highly extensible PyTorch framework supporting multiple datasets (MNIST, CIFAR-10, CASIA-HWDB), advanced architectures (ViTs, MoE, Siamese networks), and features like parameter annealing and mixed-precision training.

- HanziTiny: A highly efficient, lightweight CNN for handwritten Chinese character recognition. It includes a real-time Tkinter GUI drawing board for instant testing.

- Ferrinx: A lightweight, Rust-based ONNX inference service designed to easily turn trained models (like our Chinese character recognition models) into HTTP APIs.

We are always looking for curious minds and collaborators! Whether you are a beginner eager to learn or an experienced developer wanting to contribute to our framework or translation efforts:

**Explore**: Check out our repositories to see our handouts and code.

**Learn with us**: Follow our progress as we continue to refine our tutorials and build better tools.
